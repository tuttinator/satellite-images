What this means for the carbon concessions

The REDD+ concessions near Palangka Raya — Katingan Mentaya and Rimba Raya — sit on exactly the kind of drained peat that dominates these fires. Open the viewer, pick Active fires and drag the slider through the season, or pick Burn scars (dNBR) to see 10 m Sentinel-2 burn-severity mapping and a hectare count per concession.

Caveats

"Fire footprint" counts 1 km MODIS pixels with at least one detection; it over-states area for small fires and is a fire activity index, not burned area. MODIS misses fires under cloud and smoke, and small or smouldering peat fires. Concession boundaries in the viewer are approximate placeholders until official polygons are loaded.
